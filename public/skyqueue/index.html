Help & FAQs

Are these real flights?

No. SkyQueue is a demonstration website. Flights, times and fares are sample data and no payment is taken.

What happens if a flight is full?

You are added to the waiting list. When a confirmed passenger cancels, the first person on the waiting list automatically gets the seat.

How do I cancel?

Open Manage Booking, enter your reference and choose Cancel booking.

How do I contact support?

This demo has no live support line. Use the FAQs above for help.
